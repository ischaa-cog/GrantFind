1. Overview

What is GrantFind?

GrantFind is an easy-to-use platform that helps you discover, apply for, and manage grant opportunities for your business. Whether you're a startup founder, small business owner, or nonprofit leader, GrantFind connects you with funding opportunities that match your needs.

Key Features

  • Grant Vault – Browse a curated collection of verified grant opportunities
  • AI Grant Finder – Answer a few questions and get personalized grant recommendations
  • Application Reviewer – Get AI-powered feedback on your grant applications before submitting
  • Proposal Writer – Step-by-step AI assistance to write compelling grant proposals
  • Business Dashboard – Track all your applications and deadlines in one place
  • Email Confirmations – Receive updates about your account and applications

How GrantFind Helps You

Challenge How GrantFind Helps
"I don't know which grants I qualify for" AI Grant Finder matches you with relevant opportunities
"Writing proposals takes too long" Proposal Writer guides you section by section
"I'm not sure if my application is strong enough" Application Reviewer scores and improves your drafts
"I lose track of deadlines" Dashboard tracks all your applications and due dates

2. Who This App Is For

GrantFind is designed for:

Small Business Owners

If you run a small business and are looking for non-dilutive funding (money you don't have to pay back or give up equity for), GrantFind can help you find grants specifically for your industry and business type.

Startup Founders

Early-stage companies often need capital to grow. GrantFind helps you discover grants for innovation, technology, and new ventures.

Women-Owned Businesses

Many grants specifically support women entrepreneurs. GrantFind includes a dedicated category of grants for women-owned businesses.

Minority-Owned Businesses

GrantFind features grants designed to support minority entrepreneurs and underrepresented founders.

Veteran-Owned Businesses

Veterans transitioning to entrepreneurship can find specialized grants and funding programs through GrantFind.

Nonprofit Organizations

Organizations with a social mission can discover foundation grants and government funding opportunities.

First-Time Grant Seekers

Never applied for a grant before? GrantFind's AI tools walk you through the entire process step by step.

Before you start, gather the following information about your business:

5. Setting Up Your Profile (First-Time Setup)

When you first log in, you'll be prompted to complete your profile. This helps GrantFind recommend better grants for you.

  1. After logging in, you'll see the [Add Business] prompt

  3. Fill in your business details:

  4. Click [Save Business]

6. How to Search for Grants

GrantFind offers two ways to find grants: browsing the Grants Vault or using the AI Grant Finder.

Method 1: Browse the Grants Vault

  1. Click [Grants Vault] in the navigation menu (or on mobile, tap the menu icon and select Grants Vault)
  2. Browse the list of available grants
  3. Each grant card shows:
    • Grant name
    • Funding organization
    • Award amount
    • Deadline
    • Brief description
[SCREENSHOT HERE: Grants Vault page]

Method 2: Use the AI Grant Finder

The Grant Finder asks you questions about your business and uses AI to match you with the best opportunities.

  1. Click [Grant Finder] in the navigation menu
  2. Answer the 9-question questionnaire:
    • Business type
    • Industry
    • Location
    • Revenue
    • Employee count
    • Years in business
    • Certifications
    • Funding purpose
    • Funding amount needed
  3. Click [Find My Grants]
  4. Review your personalized list of 10 matched grants
  5. Click any grant to learn more

Quick Tip: The AI Grant Finder is the fastest way to find grants you actually qualify for. The more accurate your answers, the better your matches!

7. How to Filter & Sort Results

Filtering Grants

In the Grants Vault, use filters to narrow your search:

  1. Look for the [Filter] options at the top of the page
  2. Available filters include:
    • Category – Women-owned, Minority-owned, Veteran-owned, General, Federal, Industry-specific
    • Award Amount – Minimum and maximum funding amounts
    • Deadline – Show only grants with upcoming deadlines
    • Application Status – Open, Closing Soon, Closed
  3. Select your desired filters
  4. Results update automatically
[SCREENSHOT HERE: Filter options]

Sorting Grants

  1. Look for the [Sort By] dropdown
  2. Choose how to organize results:
    • Deadline (Soonest First) – See urgent opportunities first
    • Award Amount (Highest First) – See largest grants first
    • Award Amount (Lowest First) – See smaller, often easier-to-win grants
    • Recently Added – See newest grant opportunities
    • Rating – See highest-rated opportunities first

Combining Filters

You can use multiple filters together. For example:

  • Category: Women-Owned + Award Amount: $10,000+ + Deadline: Next 30 days

This shows women-owned business grants over $10,000 due within the next month.

Understanding Eligibility

Before applying, carefully review the eligibility section. Common requirements include:

  • Business Type – Must be an LLC, Corporation, etc.
  • Revenue Limits – Maximum annual revenue
  • Location – Must operate in certain states/cities
  • Industry – Specific sectors only
  • Age – Business must be X years old
  • Certifications – Must be woman/minority/veteran-owned

Visiting the Official Grant Page

  1. On the grant details page, find the [Apply Now] or [Official Website] button
  2. Click to open the grant program's official website in a new tab
  3. You'll complete your actual application on their website
Note: GrantFind helps you find and prepare for grants, but you submit applications directly to the grant provider's website.

9. How to Save/Bookmark Grants

Saving a Grant to Your Favorites

  1. Find a grant you're interested in
  2. Click the [Save] or [Bookmark] icon (usually a star or heart)
  3. The grant is now saved to your account

Viewing Your Saved Grants

  1. Go to your [Dashboard]
  2. Look for the [Saved Grants] section
  3. View all grants you've bookmarked

Removing a Saved Grant

  1. Go to your saved grants
  2. Click the [Save] icon again to unsave
  3. Or click [Remove] next to the grant

Organizing Saved Grants

Consider saving grants into mental categories:

  • Grants you definitely qualify for
  • Grants you might qualify for (need to verify)
  • Grants to apply for this month
  • Grants to apply for later

10. How to Track Deadlines & Reminders

Your Application Dashboard

The dashboard helps you track all your grant activities in one place.

  1. Click [Home] or the GrantFind logo to go to your dashboard
  2. View the [My Applications] section
  3. See all grants you've applied to or are preparing for

Understanding Application Status

Status Meaning
Not Started You saved the grant but haven't begun applying
In Progress You're currently working on this application
Submitted You've submitted your application
Under Review The grant provider is reviewing your application
Approved Congratulations! Your application was successful
Rejected Your application was not selected this time

Deadline Tracking

  • Deadlines appear on grant cards in the format "Due Aug 18" or "Due in 5 days"
  • Grants due within 7 days show "Due in X days" to grab your attention
  • Past deadlines are marked as "Closed"

Setting Up Reminders

  1. When viewing a grant, look for the [Set Reminder] option
  2. Choose when you want to be reminded (1 week before, 3 days before, etc.)
  3. You'll receive an email reminder before the deadline

11. How to Export or Share

Sharing a Grant with Others

  1. Open the grant details page
  2. Look for the [Share] button
  3. Options may include:
    • Copy link to clipboard
    • Share via email
    • Share on social media

Exporting Grant Information

If you need grant information for your records:

  1. Open the grant details page
  2. Use your browser's print function (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P)
  3. Select "Save as PDF" as the destination
  4. Save the file to your computer

Sharing with Team Members

If you're working with partners or team members:

  1. Copy the grant page URL from your browser
  2. Send the link via email or messaging
  3. They'll need a GrantFind account to view full details

12. Best Practices

How to Find Better Grants Faster

1. Complete Your Profile Fully

The more information you provide about your business, the better your grant matches will be.

2. Use the AI Grant Finder First

Instead of browsing hundreds of grants, let the AI narrow it down to your best 10 matches.

3. Apply to Grants You Actually Qualify For

Read eligibility requirements carefully. Applying to grants you don't qualify for wastes your time.

4. Start with Smaller Grants

Grants under $25,000 often have simpler applications and less competition. Build your grant-winning track record with these first.

5. Apply Early, Not Last Minute

Many grants review applications as they come in. Submitting early can give you an advantage.

6. Use the Proposal Writer Tool

Don't stare at a blank page. The AI Proposal Writer guides you through each section step by step.

7. Use the Application Reviewer Before Submitting

Get AI feedback on your application to catch weak spots and improve your chances.

8. Track Everything in Your Dashboard

Log every application to stay organized and never miss a deadline.

9. Reuse and Adapt

Once you write a strong grant proposal, you can adapt it for similar opportunities.

10. Apply to Multiple Grants

Don't put all your eggs in one basket. Apply to 5-10 relevant grants to increase your odds of winning.

13. Troubleshooting

Problem → Solution

Problem Solution
I can't log in 1. Check that you're using the correct email address.
2. Try the "Forgot Password" option.
3. Make sure Caps Lock is off.
4. Clear your browser cache and try again.
I'm not receiving confirmation emails 1. Check your spam/junk folder.
2. Add noreply@grantfind.com to your contacts.
3. Wait 5-10 minutes and check again.
4. Contact support if still not received.
The page won't load 1. Refresh the page (F5 or Cmd+R).
2. Try a different browser.
3. Clear your browser cache.
4. Check your internet connection.
I can't find grants for my industry 1. Try broader search terms.
2. Use the AI Grant Finder for better matches.
3. Check the "General" category.
4. Federal grants often apply to many industries.
My saved grants disappeared 1. Make sure you're logged into the correct account.
2. Check if the grant deadline has passed (expired grants may be archived).
The Apply button doesn't work 1. The link opens in a new tab – check if it's blocked by your browser.
2. Allow pop-ups for this site.
3. Try right-clicking and selecting "Open in New Tab".
I entered wrong business information 1. Go to your business profile.
2. Click "Edit" to update your information.
3. Save your changes.
The site looks broken on my phone 1. Make sure you're using the mobile menu (hamburger icon).
2. Try rotating your phone to landscape.
3. Update your mobile browser.
Grant Finder won't give me results 1. Make sure you answered all 9 questions.
2. Try different/broader answers.
3. Refresh and try again.
I got logged out unexpectedly 1. Sessions expire after inactivity.

3. Your saved data is preserved.

14. FAQ

General Questions

Q: Is GrantFind free to use?

A: GrantFind offers both free and premium features. Basic grant browsing and search are typically free. Premium features like AI tools may require a subscription.

Q: Does GrantFind guarantee I'll win a grant?

A: No. GrantFind helps you find and apply for grants, but winning depends on your business, the competition, and how well your application meets the grant criteria.

Q: How often are new grants added?

A: We regularly update our database with new opportunities. Check back frequently or use the "Recently Added" sort option.

Q: Can I use GrantFind on my phone?

A: Yes! GrantFind is fully mobile-responsive. Access it through your phone's web browser.

Account Questions

Q: Can I have multiple businesses on one account?

A: Yes! You can add multiple businesses to your account and track grants for each separately.

Q: How do I change my email address?

A: Go to your profile settings and update your email. You'll need to verify the new address.

Q: Is my information secure?

A: Yes. GrantFind uses encryption and secure connections to protect your data.

Grant Questions

Q: Are these grants real?

A: Yes. All grants in GrantFind are verified and sourced from legitimate organizations, foundations, and government programs.

Q: Do I apply through GrantFind?

A: No. GrantFind helps you find grants, but you submit applications directly on the grant provider's website.

Q: What if a grant deadline has passed?

A: Many grants have recurring deadlines. Check the official website to see when applications reopen.

Q: Can I apply for grants outside my state?

A: It depends on the grant. Some are nationwide, while others are location-specific. Check eligibility carefully.

AI Tools Questions

Q: How does the AI Grant Finder work?

A: You answer 9 questions about your business, and our AI matches you with the 10 most relevant grants from our database.

Q: How does the Application Reviewer work?

A: Paste your grant application text, and the AI scores it across 7 criteria, providing specific feedback to strengthen your application.

Q: How does the Proposal Writer work?

A: It's a 6-step wizard that guides you through writing each section of your grant proposal with AI assistance.

15. Glossary of Grant Terms

Term Definition
Award Amount The total funding available through the grant, either as a lump sum or range.
Deadline The final date by which your grant application must be submitted.
Eligibility The requirements you must meet to apply for a grant (business type, location, revenue, etc.).
Foundation Grant Funding provided by private foundations, often for specific causes or communities.
Federal Grant Funding provided by the U.S. government through agencies like the SBA.
Grant Money given to a business or organization that doesn't need to be paid back (unlike a loan).
Grant Application The forms and documents you submit to apply for a grant.
Grant Proposal A written document explaining your project, goals, and how you'll use the funding.
Letter of Intent (LOI) A brief letter expressing your interest before submitting a full application.
Match / Matching Funds Some grants require you to contribute a percentage of the project cost.
Non-Dilutive Funding Money that doesn't require giving up ownership (equity) in your business.
Nonprofit An organization that operates for a social mission rather than profit.
RFP (Request for Proposal) An announcement inviting organizations to apply for a grant or contract.
SBA Small Business Administration – a U.S. government agency supporting small businesses.
Seed Funding Early-stage money to help start a new project or business.
Small Business Generally defined as a company with fewer than 500 employees (varies by industry).
Technical Assistance Support services (training, mentoring) sometimes included with grants.
Woman-Owned Business A business that is at least 51% owned by one or more women.
Minority-Owned Business A business that is at least 51% owned by one or more minority individuals.
Veteran-Owned Business A business that is at least 51% owned by one or more military veterans.
